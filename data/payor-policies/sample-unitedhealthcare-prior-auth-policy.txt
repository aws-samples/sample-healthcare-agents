UnitedHealthcare - Prior Authorization Requirements
====================================================

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 2025

OVERVIEW
UnitedHealthcare requires prior authorization for select medical services,
procedures, and medications to ensure medical necessity and appropriate
utilization of healthcare resources.

SERVICES REQUIRING PRIOR AUTHORIZATION

Category 1: Diagnostic Imaging
- MRI (all regions): Prior auth required for all non-emergent MRI studies
  CPT Codes: 70551-70559, 72141-72158, 73218-73225
- CT with contrast: Prior auth required
  CPT Codes: 70460-70498, 72126-72133, 74160-74178
- PET/CT: Prior auth always required
  CPT Codes: 78811-78816

Category 2: Outpatient Surgical Procedures
- Orthopedic: Joint replacement, arthroscopy, spinal procedures
- Cardiac: Catheterization, stenting, bypass
- General: Hernia repair (select), cholecystectomy (select)
- Oncologic: Biopsies requiring sedation

Category 3: Specialty Pharmacy
- All biologic medications
- Specialty infusions (administered in office or home)
- Oncology oral medications (select)
- Rare disease medications

Category 4: Behavioral Health
- Inpatient psychiatric admission
- Residential treatment
- Intensive outpatient programs
- Electroconvulsive therapy (ECT)

MEDICAL NECESSITY CRITERIA FOR IMAGING

MRI Lumbar Spine (CPT 72148, 72149, 72158):
- Required: Documented back pain for minimum 4 weeks
- Required: Failed conservative treatment (NSAIDs, physical therapy)
- Required: Neurological symptoms (radiculopathy, weakness, numbness)
  OR red flag symptoms (bowel/bladder dysfunction, progressive weakness)
- Supporting: Previous X-ray results
- Supporting: Physical therapy notes

MRI Brain (CPT 70551, 70553):
- Required: Clinical indication (headache with red flags, neurological deficit,
  seizure, suspected mass)
- Required: Neurological examination documented
- Supporting: Previous imaging if available

CT Abdomen/Pelvis (CPT 74177, 74178):
- Required: Clinical indication documented
- Required: Relevant lab results (if applicable)
- Supporting: Previous imaging results

DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

Minimum required documentation:
1. Prior authorization request form (electronic preferred)
2. Office visit notes from ordering physician (within 30 days)
3. Relevant diagnostic results (labs, imaging)
4. Treatment history showing conservative measures
5. ICD-10 diagnosis codes
6. CPT procedure codes
7. Rendering provider NPI
8. Facility NPI (if applicable)

TURNAROUND TIMES
- Standard: 5 business days
- Urgent: 24 hours
- Concurrent (inpatient): Same day
- Retrospective: 30 calendar days
