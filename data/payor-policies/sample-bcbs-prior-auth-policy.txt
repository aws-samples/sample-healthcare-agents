Blue Cross Blue Shield - Prior Authorization Policy Guidelines
==============================================================

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 2025

SECTION 1: PROCEDURES REQUIRING PRIOR AUTHORIZATION

The following categories of services require prior authorization before
the service is rendered:

1.1 Advanced Imaging
- MRI (all body regions): CPT 70551-70559, 72141-72158, 73218-73225, 74181-74183
- CT Scan (all body regions): CPT 70450-70498, 72125-72133, 74150-74178
- PET Scan: CPT 78811-78816
- Nuclear Medicine: CPT 78300-78399

1.2 Surgical Procedures
- Joint Replacement (hip, knee, shoulder): CPT 27130, 27447, 23472
- Spinal Surgery: CPT 22551-22558, 22612-22614, 63001-63048
- Bariatric Surgery: CPT 43644, 43645, 43770-43775
- Cardiac Surgery: CPT 33510-33536

1.3 Specialty Medications
- Biologic agents (all)
- Specialty infusion medications
- Gene therapy
- Oncology medications (select)

1.4 Durable Medical Equipment (DME)
- Power wheelchairs and scooters
- CPAP/BiPAP machines
- Home oxygen therapy
- Prosthetic devices over $1,000

SECTION 2: MEDICAL NECESSITY CRITERIA

2.1 Advanced Imaging (MRI/CT)
Prior authorization for advanced imaging requires:
- Clinical indication documented by ordering physician
- Conservative treatment attempted for minimum 4-6 weeks (non-emergent)
- Previous imaging results reviewed (if applicable)
- Diagnosis code supporting the imaging study

2.2 Joint Replacement Surgery
Prior authorization requires:
- Documented failure of conservative treatment (minimum 3 months)
- Physical therapy attempted (minimum 6 weeks)
- BMI documented (BMI > 40 may require additional review)
- Pre-operative imaging (X-ray within 6 months)
- Orthopedic specialist evaluation

2.3 Spinal Surgery
Prior authorization requires:
- MRI or CT imaging within 6 months
- Conservative treatment for minimum 6 weeks
- Physical therapy documentation
- Pain management consultation
- Neurological examination findings

SECTION 3: DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

All prior authorization requests must include:
1. Completed prior authorization request form
2. Clinical notes from the ordering/referring physician
3. Relevant diagnostic test results
4. Treatment history and conservative measures attempted
5. ICD-10 diagnosis code(s)
6. CPT/HCPCS procedure code(s)
7. Expected date of service
8. Facility/provider information

SECTION 4: SUBMISSION PROCESS

- Electronic submission preferred via provider portal
- Fax submissions accepted at 1-800-XXX-XXXX
- Standard review: 5-7 business days
- Urgent review: 24-72 hours (requires clinical justification)
- Emergency: Retrospective review within 48 hours of service

SECTION 5: APPEAL PROCESS

If a prior authorization request is denied:
- Level 1 Appeal: Submit within 30 days with additional documentation
- Level 2 Appeal: Peer-to-peer review with medical director
- External Review: Available after exhausting internal appeals
