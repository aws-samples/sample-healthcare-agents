# Clinical Evidence Guidelines for Appeals
# Reference for Appeals Agent — Medical Necessity Justification

## Medical Necessity Criteria Frameworks

### InterQual Criteria (Cambio Health Solutions)
- Evidence-based clinical decision support criteria
- Used by many commercial payers for utilization review
- Covers: Acute care, procedures, imaging, DME, rehabilitation
- Appeal Strategy: Document how patient meets each InterQual criterion
- Key Elements: Severity of illness, intensity of service, clinical indicators

### MCG (Milliman Care Guidelines)
- Evidence-based care guidelines used by payers
- Covers: Inpatient, outpatient, ambulatory, recovery facility
- Appeal Strategy: Map clinical findings to specific MCG criteria
- Key Elements: Clinical indications, contraindications, alternative treatments tried

### Hayes Health Technology Assessment
- Technology assessment for new/emerging procedures
- Appeal Strategy: Reference Hayes rating for procedure effectiveness
- Ratings: A (established), B (promising), C (unclear), D1/D2 (insufficient)

## Common Denial Categories and Clinical Evidence Requirements

### Advanced Imaging (MRI, CT, PET)
- Required Evidence:
  - Failed conservative treatment documentation (duration, type)
  - Physical examination findings
  - Prior imaging results (X-ray before MRI)
  - Clinical symptoms and functional limitations
  - Red flag symptoms warranting urgent imaging
- Key Guidelines:
  - ACR Appropriateness Criteria for imaging selection
  - AAN guidelines for neurological imaging
  - Choosing Wisely recommendations

### Joint Replacement Surgery
- Required Evidence:
  - Duration of symptoms (typically 3-6 months minimum)
  - Failed conservative treatments (PT, NSAIDs, injections)
  - Functional limitation documentation (validated scores)
  - Imaging showing joint degeneration (Kellgren-Lawrence grade)
  - BMI documentation (some payers require BMI below threshold)
- Key Guidelines:
  - AAOS Clinical Practice Guidelines for knee/hip OA
  - ACR/AAHKS guidelines for total joint arthroplasty
- Validated Outcome Measures:
  - WOMAC (Western Ontario and McMaster Universities Osteoarthritis Index)
  - KOOS (Knee Injury and Osteoarthritis Outcome Score)
  - Harris Hip Score

### Spinal Surgery
- Required Evidence:
  - Duration of symptoms (typically 6-12 weeks minimum)
  - Failed conservative treatment (PT, medications, epidural injections)
  - Neurological examination findings
  - Imaging correlation (MRI showing nerve compression)
  - Functional limitation documentation
  - Psychological evaluation (for fusion procedures)
- Key Guidelines:
  - NASS (North American Spine Society) Clinical Guidelines
  - AAN guidelines for lumbar disc herniation
  - AANS/CNS guidelines for degenerative lumbar conditions

### Specialty Medications (Biologics)
- Required Evidence:
  - Diagnosis confirmation with supporting labs/imaging
  - Failed conventional therapy documentation (drug, dose, duration, reason for failure)
  - Step therapy compliance documentation
  - Disease activity scores (DAS28, CDAI for RA; PASI for psoriasis)
  - Contraindications to alternative medications
- Key Guidelines:
  - ACR guidelines for rheumatoid arthritis treatment
  - AGA guidelines for inflammatory bowel disease
  - AAD guidelines for psoriasis management

### Inpatient Admission
- Required Evidence:
  - Severity of illness indicators (vital signs, lab values)
  - Intensity of service requirements (IV medications, monitoring frequency)
  - Risk of adverse outcome without admission
  - Failed outpatient management documentation
  - Comorbidities increasing risk
- Key Guidelines:
  - InterQual Acute Criteria
  - MCG Inpatient/Surgical Care Criteria
  - CMS Two-Midnight Rule (Medicare)

### Rehabilitation Services (PT/OT/Speech)
- Required Evidence:
  - Functional baseline assessment with validated measures
  - Measurable treatment goals with expected timeframes
  - Progress documentation showing functional improvement
  - Skilled care justification (why services require licensed therapist)
  - Home exercise program compliance
- Key Guidelines:
  - APTA Clinical Practice Guidelines
  - Medicare therapy caps and exceptions process
  - CMS Improvement Standard (Jimmo v. Sebelius)
- Validated Outcome Measures:
  - FIM (Functional Independence Measure)
  - Timed Up and Go (TUG)
  - Berg Balance Scale
  - AM-PAC (Activity Measure for Post-Acute Care)

### Durable Medical Equipment (DME)
- Required Evidence:
  - Diagnosis supporting medical necessity
  - Functional limitation documentation
  - Home assessment (for mobility equipment)
  - Face-to-face encounter documentation
  - Prescription with specific equipment details
- Key Guidelines:
  - Medicare LCD for specific DME categories
  - CMS DME face-to-face requirements
  - Supplier standards and accreditation requirements

## Peer-to-Peer Review Preparation

### Before the Call
- Review complete medical record and denial reason
- Prepare chronological clinical summary
- Identify specific criteria the patient meets
- Have relevant guidelines and literature citations ready
- Know the payer's specific coverage policy for the service

### During the Call
- Present clinical case concisely (2-3 minutes)
- Address each denial reason specifically
- Reference clinical guidelines by name and recommendation
- Emphasize failed alternatives and clinical urgency
- Document the reviewer's name, credentials, and decision
- Request written confirmation of outcome

### After the Call
- Document call details in patient record
- If denied, request written denial with specific criteria not met
- Proceed to formal written appeal if peer-to-peer unsuccessful
