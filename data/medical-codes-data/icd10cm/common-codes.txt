Code: I10
Title: Essential (primary) hypertension
Category: Hypertensive diseases
Description: High blood pressure without identified secondary cause
Clinical Context: Chronic elevation of blood pressure, typically >140/90 mmHg. Most common form of hypertension.
Related Codes: I11 (Hypertensive heart disease), I12 (Hypertensive chronic kidney disease), I13 (Hypertensive heart and chronic kidney disease)
Includes: High blood pressure, HBP, HTN, Arterial hypertension

Code: E11.9
Title: Type 2 diabetes mellitus without complications
Category: Diabetes mellitus
Description: Non-insulin dependent diabetes mellitus without complications
Clinical Context: Adult-onset diabetes, typically managed with oral medications and lifestyle modifications. Blood glucose consistently elevated.
Related Codes: E11.0-E11.8 (Type 2 diabetes with complications), E10 (Type 1 diabetes)
Includes: Diabetes NOS, NIDDM, Adult-onset diabetes

Code: I21.0
Title: ST elevation (STEMI) myocardial infarction of anterior wall
Category: Acute myocardial infarction
Description: Acute heart attack with ST segment elevation affecting anterior wall
Clinical Context: Life-threatening condition requiring immediate intervention. ECG shows ST elevation in anterior leads (V1-V4).
Related Codes: I21.1 (Inferior wall MI), I21.2 (Other sites), I21.3 (Transmural MI), I21.4 (Non-ST elevation MI)
Excludes: Old myocardial infarction (I25.2)

Title: Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, unspecified
Category: Chronic lower respiratory diseases
