Payer: Medicare
Rule: NPI Required
Description: NPI required for all providers. Taxonomy code must match specialty. Medicare Beneficiary Identifier (MBI) required, not SSN.

---

Payer: Medicare
Rule: Timely Filing
Description: Claims must be filed within 1 year of date of service.

---

Payer: Medicare
Rule: Prior Authorization
Description: Prior authorization required when applicable. Authorization number in REF segment of Loop 2300.

---

Payer: Medicaid
Rule: State-Specific Requirements
Description: State-specific Medicaid ID required. Timely filing varies by state (typically 90-365 days). Prior auth required for many services.

---

Payer: Medicaid
Rule: Coordination of Benefits
Description: Coordination of benefits information required if other insurance exists. Patient relationship to subscriber required.

---

Payer: Blue Cross Blue Shield
Rule: Required Fields
Description: NPI, Tax ID (EIN), group number, member ID required. Timely filing: 180 days. Prior auth number when applicable.

---

Payer: Blue Cross Blue Shield
Rule: Modifiers
Description: Modifier usage per BCBS guidelines. Service facility NPI required. Referring provider information when required.

---

Payer: UnitedHealthcare
Rule: Required Fields
Description: NPI for billing and rendering, Tax ID, member ID, group number required. Timely filing: 180 days from date of service.

---

Payer: UnitedHealthcare
Rule: Diagnosis Codes
Description: ICD-10-CM required, up to 12 diagnosis codes. Prior authorization reference number required when applicable.

---

Payer: Aetna
Rule: Required Fields
Description: NPI for all providers, Tax ID for billing entity, member ID required. Timely filing: 180 days. Prior auth when required.

---

Payer: Aetna
Rule: Rendering Provider
Description: Rendering provider information required. Service facility NPI required. Modifier usage per Aetna guidelines.

---

Payer: Cigna
Rule: Required Fields
Description: NPI for billing and rendering, Tax ID, member ID, group number required. Timely filing: 180 days.

---

Payer: All Payers
Rule: Common Required Fields
Description: Patient demographics (name, DOB, gender, address), subscriber info, provider NPI/name/address, service dates, diagnoses, procedures, charges.
