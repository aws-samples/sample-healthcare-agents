Rule: ISA/GS/ST Segments
Type: EDI 837P Structure
Description: Transaction envelope: ISA (interchange), GS (functional group), ST (transaction set header 837).

---

Rule: BHT Segment
Type: EDI 837P Structure
Description: Beginning of Hierarchical Transaction. Required. Identifies transaction purpose: original, replacement, void.

---

Rule: Loop 2000A
Type: EDI 837P Structure
Description: Billing Provider Hierarchical Level. Required. Contains HL, PRV, NM1, N3, N4, REF (tax ID), PER segments.

---

Rule: Loop 2000B
Type: EDI 837P Structure
Description: Subscriber Hierarchical Level. Required. Contains HL, SBR, NM1 (subscriber name + member ID), N3, N4, DMG.

---

Rule: Loop 2000C
Type: EDI 837P Structure
Description: Patient Level. Required only when patient is not the subscriber. Contains HL, PAT, NM1, N3, N4, DMG.

---

Rule: Loop 2300 CLM Segment
Type: EDI 837P Structure
Description: Claim Information. Required. Fields: claim ID, total charge, place of service, signature indicator, assignment of benefits.

---

Rule: Loop 2300 HI Segment
Type: EDI 837P Structure
Description: Diagnosis codes. Required. Principal diagnosis in first position. Up to 12 ICD-10-CM codes total.

---

Rule: Loop 2400 SV1 Segment
Type: EDI 837P Structure
Description: Professional Service per line. Required. Fields: CPT/HCPCS code, up to 4 modifiers, charge, units, place of service.

---

Rule: NPI Format
Type: Field Validation
Description: National Provider Identifier must be 10 numeric digits. Required for billing, rendering, referring providers. Must be active in NPPES.

---

Rule: Tax ID (EIN) Format
Type: Field Validation
Description: 9 numeric digits in format XX-XXXXXXX. Required for billing provider. Must match IRS records.

---

Rule: Member ID Format
Type: Field Validation
Description: Alphanumeric, payer-specific format. No special characters. Must be active on date of service.

---

Rule: Diagnosis Code Format
Type: Field Validation
Description: ICD-10-CM: 3-7 alphanumeric characters. Minimum 1, maximum 12. Must be valid and support medical necessity.

---

Rule: Procedure Code Format
Type: Field Validation
Description: CPT: 5 numeric digits. HCPCS: 1 letter + 4 digits. Must be valid and current. Must match place of service.

---

Rule: Modifier Format
Type: Field Validation
Description: 2 alphanumeric characters. Maximum 4 per procedure. Must be valid CPT/HCPCS modifier for that procedure.

---

Rule: Place of Service
Type: Field Validation
Description: 2 numeric digits 01-99. Common: 11 Office, 21 Inpatient, 22 Outpatient, 23 ER, 31 SNF, 81 Lab.

---

Rule: Date Format
Type: Field Validation
Description: CCYYMMDD (8 digits). Service date must not be future. Must be within coverage period and timely filing limits.

---

Rule: Charge and Units
Type: Field Validation
Description: Charges: decimal with 2 places, must be >0. Units must be >0. Total claim charge must equal sum of line charges.

---

Rule: Prior Authorization
Type: Business Rule
Description: REF segment in Loop 2300. Required when payer mandates authorization. Must match approved authorization number.

---

Rule: Duplicate Claim Check
Type: Business Rule
Description: Claim ID must be unique per submitter. Same patient, provider, date of service, and procedure = duplicate.

---

Rule: Timely Filing
Type: Business Rule
Description: Service date must be within payer timely filing limit. Medicare: 1 year. Commercial payers: typically 180 days.

---

Rule: Medical Necessity
Type: Business Rule
Description: Diagnosis codes must support medical necessity for all procedures. Principal diagnosis must be most resource-intensive.

---

Rule: Pre-Submission Checklist
Type: Validation
Description: Verify: all required loops present, required segments populated, field formats correct, codes valid, cross-field validations pass.
